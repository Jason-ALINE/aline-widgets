Step-by-step procedure

1

Familiarize with terms, techniques, and procedures Follow all safety rules

Understand the vocabulary, tools, and methods before beginning. Review the introduction for a full overview of reverse dial alignment principles.

Introduction to alignment
2

Learn about the machine you are aligning

  • Visually check coupling, pipe hangers, base bolts, and coupling spacing
  • Check for coupling and shaft run-out
3

Know the characteristics of your tool

Perform a sag check to understand your indicator's deflection under its own weight before taking any measurements.

Sag check instructions
4

Prepare the machine

  • Remove all existing shims — if reusing old shims, clean thoroughly and use the minimum amount possible
  • Clean the base thoroughly: scrape and file away all rust, nicks, and burrs
  • Examine base bolts and holes — re-tap or replace as necessary
5

Install alignment brackets

  • Clean the mounting surface and file off any nicks and burrs
  • Check indicators for sticking and loose needles
  • Aim the indicator stem directly toward the centerline of the shaft
6

Take measurements

  • Measure the distance between the two indicators
  • Measure the distance between the indicator and front feet
  • Measure the distance between front and back feet
7

Lay out graph paper

  • Mark indicator positions
  • Mark feet positions — include + and − signs to eliminate confusion

Example: graph layout for indicator and feet positions

8

Preliminary horizontal move

Using your graph layout, determine and perform the initial horizontal correction before proceeding to other checks.

Horizontal move instructions
9

Check for soft foot

Identify and correct any soft foot condition before performing the vertical move. Skipping this step can cause inaccurate final readings.

Soft foot check instructions
10

Perform the vertical move

Add or remove shims at the machine feet to achieve the correct vertical alignment as determined by your graph calculations.

Vertical move instructions
11

Tighten all bolts and recheck indicator readings

Once the machine is in position, torque all base bolts to spec and verify that indicator readings remain within tolerance.

12

Remove alignment brackets

Carefully remove all alignment brackets and store them properly. The alignment job is complete.

The more familiar you become with these steps, the faster and more confidently you will be able to align your machinery.
